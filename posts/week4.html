REROLL

Borders can be unstable

Near-equal modularity can hide different memberships. A single Louvain run looks more certain than it is.

THE TAKEAWAY

A community map is a lens, not a label-maker. Trust the structures that survive changes in scale, seed, and edge filtering.

What we did

We load the frozen Week 4 philosopher snapshot, sum both directions into one weighted undirected graph, and analyze its giant component. Community partitions use Louvain modularity with an adjustable resolution γ. Modularity is recomputed from the displayed partition rather than treated as a measure of truth.

For stability, we run Louvain five times at γ = 1 with different seeded random orders. A philosopher’s “loyalty” is the average Jaccard similarity between its community in the first run and its community in the other runs. For the weighted X-ray, an edge is kept when its disparity-filter significance is below α at either endpoint.

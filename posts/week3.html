High degree and high betweenness often overlap, but not perfectly. The outliers are the interesting brokers.

If a metric claims to identify structurally important characters, targeted removals let us test that claim directly.

YOUR TURN

What we did

We load the same frozen course snapshot used in Weeks 1 and 2, collapse directed links to undirected pairs, and analyze the giant component. Degree, closeness, eigenvector centrality, and Brandes betweenness are computed in the browser. Shortest paths use breadth-first search.

The collapse experiment removes characters according to a fixed ranking by degree, betweenness, closeness, or a reproducible random order, recalculating the surviving giant component after each removal. Maximal cliques are found with a Bron–Kerbosch search and filtered interactively by size.
